Guide — how to use this tracker
The goal · Consistency over volume
🌿
The best muraja'ah is the one you can sustain for life. Even a single juz daily — held consistently across months and years — is worth far more than a heavy load that burns out in weeks. This tracker is built around that principle: protect the habit first, then grow it.
Getting started · Build your pool
📖
Add only the juz you have memorised and are ready to revise. Go to the ⚙ Juz tab and add each juz to your active pool. A starting pool of 5–10 juz at 2 juz/day gives a healthy 3–5 day rotation — close enough to keep each juz fresh in your memory.
Daily reading · The rotation system
🔄
The tracker assigns your juz automatically — just open Today and read. Your active pool is divided evenly across a rolling cycle: 2 juz/day for pools up to 19 juz, and 3 juz/day once your pool reaches 20. You do not need to plan what to read each day — the Rotation tab shows the full schedule at a glance.
Growing your pool · Add gradually
📈
Add 1–2 new juz every 2–4 weeks, only when each feels solid. A juz is ready for your pool when you can recite it confidently without prompting. Never rush this — adding juz before they are firm creates stress rather than progress. Use the ⚙ Juz tab to add at any time; the rotation updates automatically.
Bonus reading · The ½ juz feature
◐
Use the ½ Juz picker for any reading beyond your daily quota. On lighter days, during travel, or whenever you feel motivated, log a half-juz from any juz 1–30 — even juz outside your active pool. The Monthly tab tracks these accurately: two halves of the same juz count as one complete juz, while an unpaired half shows as ½.
Readiness signals · When to do more
✅
Consider increasing your pool size or daily quota when: (1) you have maintained a 14+ day streak at your current load without strain, (2) your daily reading feels effortless rather than effortful, and (3) your schedule has genuine capacity for more. Meeting all three is ideal — but two out of three is enough to take the next step.
Skipped days · Log honestly
📅
Record skipped days rather than leaving them blank. The streak counter and monthly overview only give you an accurate picture when skips are logged. A skipped day is not a failure — it is information. Patterns in your log reveal when life is working against the habit, and that is when it is time to adjust, not abandon.
A note on intention
🤲
This tracker is a means, not an end. Numbers and streaks are useful only insofar as they serve your connection with the Quran. On days when life is heavy, even sitting with the mushaf for a few minutes has its own barakah — log what you can and let Allah complete the rest.
